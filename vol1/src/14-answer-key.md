# Answer Key

Answers to every practice question in this book, and to the full-length mock exam, are collected here.

Work through a chapter’s questions without turning to this section. Note your answers, then check them in a batch. If you score below roughly 70% on a chapter’s questions, re-read that chapter before moving on — the real exam’s passing bar is 720 out of 1000, which is close to the same proportion.

Each answer gives the correct option and an explanation covering both why it is right and why the most tempting wrong option fails. The explanations of the wrong answers are often the more useful half.
