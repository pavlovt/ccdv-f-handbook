# Resources

This page lists where to go next, grouped by source. Annotations say what each resource is for and when it is useful. Links are given only where they are verified. Where a page has no URL in this book, the entry says how to find it.

## Official Anthropic

**platform.claude.com** (the older docs.claude.com links redirect here) — The primary source for the Claude API, MCP, and Claude Code. Use it to verify every API detail in this book, because parameters, model names, and SDK syntax change. When this book and the documentation disagree, the documentation is current and this book is not.

**anthropic.com/learn** — The catalog for Anthropic Academy, Anthropic’s free learning platform. It is open to any email address and lists around 21 free courses. Start here if you cannot register for the partner exam, and work through the developer courses on the API, MCP, and Claude Code, which map directly onto Chapters 3, 9, and 10.

**anthropic.skilljar.com** — The course platform behind Anthropic Academy. Courses are free, and you can take them with a personal email address. Create an account with any address and work through them in order.

**anthropic-partners.skilljar.com** — The partner academy, which hosts the official prep course. It is gated: you need employment at a CPN organization and a registered company email on a recognized domain to access it. If you are not eligible, do not spend time trying to get in. Use the free courses above.

**Certifications FAQ** (anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications) — The authoritative source on eligibility, pricing and partner-tier discounts, scoring, retake waiting periods, recertification, and name corrections. Read it before you register. Note that the page itself sits behind the partner login, so you may not be able to open it until your access is sorted.

**Pearson VUE** (pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html) — Scheduling, rescheduling, score reports, and the network domain allowlist your IT team may need if you test on a managed corporate device. The online-proctoring requirements and system test live at pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic/onvue.html; run the system test well before exam day, and check the accepted forms of identification under the proof-of-identification tab.

**Claude Certification Program** (anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications) — The hub for all four certifications. Each certification page carries its exam guide, prep courses, and registration link. The CCDV-F page itself is at anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/claude-certified-developer-foundations-certification, and the official five-course prep track is at anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/path/claude-certified-developer-foundations. All three are partner-gated.

**Official Exam Guide v1.0 (July 2026)** — The authoritative scope document for CCDV-F. It sits behind the partner login. If you can access it, it overrides every domain weight and topic list in this book.

## Suggested order

If you are eligible, start with the partner course and the Exam Guide. If you are not, work in this order: the Anthropic Academy developer courses, then the matching chapters of this book, then the documentation at platform.claude.com for anything that needs exact parameters. Return to the documentation whenever a practice question depends on a detail you are not sure of, because that is the detail the exam will test.

## Contacts

**partner-support\@anthropic.com** — Use this to request domain linking for your company email. Domain linking takes 7–10 days, so start early if you are eligible but your domain is not yet linked.

**certifications-support\@anthropic.com** — Use this for name corrections on your Pearson VUE profile and for refund questions. Name corrections must be requested at least 24 hours before your appointment, and they take 24–48 business hours to process.

## Caution: exam dumps and unreliable study material

**Exam “dumps” sites are unreliable and likely violate certification policy.** Sites that advertise “real exam questions” or “recent brain dumps” share content that is taken from the live exam. That content is frequently wrong, and it is usually obtained in a way that breaks the program’s confidentiality terms. Using it can put your certification at risk, and it does nothing for your ability to build production systems on Claude. Do not buy or use it.

**Check third-party study material against the exam code before you buy it.** One low-cost third-party study eBook on the market shipped with a product description that confused the Associate and Developer certifications. Those are different exams, and the mix-up matters if you buy material for the wrong one. Treat that kind of error as a signal of how carefully the material was assembled. Read its scope against the Exam Guide before you pay, and prefer the free Anthropic documentation and courses as your baseline.
