# Answer Keys

All twelve keys follow, in exam order.

Finish an exam before opening its key. If you are part-way through and tempted to check one answer, that temptation is worth noticing: it usually means the question found something you are unsure about, which is exactly what you want to discover under timed conditions rather than on exam day.

Mark your paper honestly. A question you guessed and got right counts as wrong for diagnostic purposes — note it, and treat it as a gap.

Each entry gives the correct option, the domain, and an explanation. For calculation questions the arithmetic is shown in full so you can find where your own working diverged rather than simply learning the answer.

After checking a key, fill in its per-domain scoring table before moving on. The closing chapter shows how to read those tables across several exams, which is where the real diagnostic value is.
